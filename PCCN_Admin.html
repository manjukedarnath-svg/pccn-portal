PICU nurse competency self-assessment

Stage I: any nurse scores herself 1–3 across 12 competencies (no login, about 5 minutes). Name directors from a learner's record (Learners tab → Make hospital course director); each sees only submissions from their own hospital. Admins see every hospital.
