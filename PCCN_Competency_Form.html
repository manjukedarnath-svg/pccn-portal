PICU Nurse Competency
Self-Assessment

Open to any PICU nurse — no course registration or login needed. Stage I of three: score yourself 1–3 in each of the 12 categories. Your unit head verifies these at Stage II before any competency is allocated.
About you
Name, hospital and email are required so your unit head can find your declaration and arrange Stage II. Everything else is optional.
What the three levels mean
Level 1 · Needs hand-holding — I know the theory but cannot act alone; a senior must be beside me directing each step.
Level 2 · Needs supervision — I do the routine case correctly, but need a senior available and checking for decisions and deterioration.
Level 3 · Independent — safe and consistent alone, including the deteriorating child, and I can teach a junior.
Score your weakest skill in each category, and claim only what you have actually done at the bedside. This is not a test — an over-claim is corrected at Stage II and only delays you.
